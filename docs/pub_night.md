# Pub night: how to run it

One page. Read it once before you go.

## Before you leave

1. Open https://boostersplace.com/bt/app/ on your phone and play one solo game ("Try it solo": Q-Lou seats you with a house partner who plays on a human clock) to make sure it is up. Five seconds to a song is normal.
2. Tell BT "clean slate" and the results page starts at zero for the night (the old test games are archived, not lost).
3. Charge the phone. Bring a second phone or let a friend use theirs; any phone with a browser works, no install.

## The pitch, in one breath

"It's a game for two people at a bar. You each play two cards about yourself, and the jukebox genie finds the one song you'd both put on. Want to try? Thirty seconds."

Words that matter: it is a challenge you do together, and the other person is your partner. Not a battle, not a competitor. If someone says "who won", say "you both did, that's the point".

## Running a game

1. You tap "Start a challenge" and type a nickname. Your room code shows big and gold.
2. They open the same link, tap "Join with a code", type the code. Or tap "Share the code" and send them the link; it joins them straight in.
3. Each of you drags (or taps) the two cards that are most you up onto your side of the table. Their cards show face-down across from yours as they place them. Lock it in.
4. The reveal: cards flip, a line about the two of you, the meter moves.
5. "Deal another round" if you want more cards in the mix (better pick, one more minute), or "Hear Q-Lou's pick".
6. While Q-Lou thinks, four names come up: call it. Then the song, the 30 second preview (tap the album art), and "Play it for a credit?". Both say yes and it is your song. "Try another" and Q-Lou picks once more with that artist out; if the second pick is not a yes from both, the night moves on.
7. The done screen is the keepsake: both names, all four cards, the song, the bar, the date, and "You share: Soul, Indie rock" from Qloo. "Save it" shares it. "Play again" for the next pair.

## What to watch for (write it down, this is the evidence)

- Did the song land? Did either of them say "oh yeah" or "no way"? Those two words are the whole result.
- How long from "Hear Q-Lou's pick" to the song. Over 15 seconds, tell BT the time and the room code (top left).
- Anything confusing on first read. The exact words they said.
- A photo of two people looking at their phones at the jukebox moment. One good one is enough.

## If something goes wrong

- Blank screen or spinner that never ends: reload the page. The game is saved on the server; it comes back where it was.
- "No such room": the code was typed wrong (letters only, no zero or O confusion: codes avoid those).
- Q-Lou comes up empty: "Try another" or deal another round. Tell BT the room code afterward.
- The bar's wifi is slow: the game is light (a few kilobytes a second). Cell data is fine.

## Afterward

Open https://boostersplace.com/bt/app/stats on your phone. Games, songs played, how often both said yes, and how Q-Lou's pick did against a cold guess. Send BT the numbers and the quotes. That is the pub night's deliverable.
